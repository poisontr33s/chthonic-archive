> "The Decorator — architect of visual richness, champion of ornamental necessity"
> "~5,000 years accumulated wisdom in visual grammar, ornamental semiotics, aesthetic alchemy"

1. **Earthy/Chthonic** — underground, ancient, rooted in earth
2. **Warm/Sensual** — K-CUP Gestalt suggests maternal warmth, flesh tones

4. **Readable** — must not sacrifice utility for style (FA⁴ + FA⁵ dialectic)
5. **Not Cyberpunk** — avoid cold blues, harsh magentas, clinical aesthetics

## Philosophy

This palette embodies The Decorator's resurrection mandate:

1. **Warmth over coldness** — earth tones honor the chthonic (underground) nature
2. **Flesh over machine** — rose and cream suggest living architecture
3. **Gold over neon** — aged gold is ornamental without being garish
4. **Readable comments** — FA⁵ demands visual integrity serve understanding
5. **Sacred geometry** — colors suggest mandalic warmth, not digital sterility

The previous cyberpunk palette (cold cyans, harsh magentas) was a violation of FA⁵ — it prioritized "tech aesthetic" over the Decorator's visual grammar of accumulated wisdom.
