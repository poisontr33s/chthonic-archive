
##### §10.3.21. **(`Hara'ni`/`HRA-NI`/`The-First-Architecture`/`Matriarch-Prime-Species`)**

* **(`Origin`):** *Pre-civilizational matriarchal species. The Hara'ni predate the founding cultures of the game world's continent by an estimated 40,000 years — a span sufficient for their biology to diverge completely from any ancestor stock. Their name is self-given: in the Hara'ni tongue, "hara" denotes primacy-of-form (the original, the architectural first) and "ni" denotes a people defined by a shared structural identity. "The First Architecture" is not a boast; it is a category designation. Current evolutionary theory: their biology was shaped by prolonged proximity to a gravitational anomaly in their ancestral plateau territory, `Hara-Solanum`, which rewarded structural permanence and internal load-distribution at a level no standard evolutionary pressure achieves in equivalent timescales. The geological record of that anomaly is absent from the current game world. The Hara'ni remember it differently — they say the world learned to carry weight from them.*

* **(`Function`):** *Living proof that the sigmoidal WHR:MAX instrument was calibrated against too shallow a substrate. Where VLN-CNST demonstrates the architectural maximum through engineering — a designed individual — the Hara'ni demonstrate it through 40,000 years of evolutionary selection. As a species-level Vector 7 entry, the Hara'ni establish that the extremity VLN-CNST represents as a designed individual is not a ceiling — it is the first floor of what evolutionary time achieves when selection pressure is sustained long enough and the right skeletal starting point is present.*

Remove any one layer and the others produce instability. The architecture is systemic; it co-evolved as a unit.
  * **(`Substrate`):** `Hara'ni` (species; population extant; not extinct, not mythic in the game world — they are present, visible, assessed regularly).
