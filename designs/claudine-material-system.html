Material Principle

Obsidian, not frosted glass. The common glassmorphism approach uses light diffusion — white surfaces, soft blur, iOS warmth. This system inverts it: dark transparency that reveals depth below the surface rather than hiding complexity behind it.

When OS-level Mica is active (Windows 11 DWM compositor), the depth tokens become partially transparent and the desktop wallpaper bleeds through as the material base. The fire and text tokens remain fully opaque — they float above the depth, never inside it.

When Mica is absent, the same tokens render as opaque obsidian with correct WCAG AA contrast. The design system works either way; vibrancy is enhancement, not dependency.

3 Raised Elements Cards, Hand bar, token swatches · --claudine-surface-raised

Patina — Time as Material Property

Stratigraphy, not weathering. In archaeology, older layers are below — deeper, darker, more compressed. The patina system maps mtime to material depth: a recently-touched leaf sits at stratum 3, fully present. An ancient leaf sinks toward stratum 1, more transparent, more behind the obsidian.

The patina tint (#4A3A28) is applied as a color-mix with the surface token, proportional to elapsed time. The leaf doesn't look old — it looks deep.
