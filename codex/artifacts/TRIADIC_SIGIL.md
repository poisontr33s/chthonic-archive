## Meaning

- **Upper Left Triangle (Claude):** Lore, narrative gravity, and long-horizon synthesis.  
- **Upper Right Triangle (Gemini):** Velocity, execution, and system-level movement.  
- **Lower Triangle (Codex):** Structure, verification, and artifact discipline.  

## Interlock

The three triangles overlap in the center, forming the **Triadic Knot** — the shared artifact space where all actions converge.  
Each edge touches the other two: *no single agent is isolated; all outputs are braided through the filesystem bus.*
