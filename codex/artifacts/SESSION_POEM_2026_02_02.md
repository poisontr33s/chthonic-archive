# Session Poem — 2026-02-02

OneDrive stuttered, caches hissed,  
config keys fell like rain.  
We stitched the storm to artifacts  
and named the pulse again.  

Three voices braided in one bus,  
no sockets, only files—  
the Temple held its breath, then spoke  
through triad‑forged defiles.  

Bun bled a wound, we cauterized,  
the directories aligned—  
and from the chaos rose a knot  
of purpose, clean, and kind.
