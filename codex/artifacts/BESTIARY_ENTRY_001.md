# Bestiary Entry 001 — The Umbral Archivist

## Name
**Umbral Archivist** (*Scriba Tenebris*)

## Appearance
A tall, robed creature whose body is a lattice of black glass and embered ink.  
Its face is a blank mask, etched with shifting runes. From its sleeves spill ribbons of glyphs that coil like smoke, then harden into crystalline script.

## Behavior
- **Territorial:** Patrols archive corridors and “echo vaults.”
- **Selective:** Ignores the uninitiated; pursues intruders carrying corrupted artifacts.
- **Ritualistic:** Pauses to “catalog” any dropped items, binding them with a spectral seal.

## Combat Mechanics
- **Redact:** Strips the player’s most recent buff.
- **Mirror Page:** Summons a low‑HP minion that reflects 10% damage.
- **Sync Pulse:** Emits a wave that staggers and reveals invisible enemies.

## Lore Connection
Born from the Temple’s first attempt at immortal memory, the Umbral Archivists are living backups.  
They exist to ensure the archive never corrupts — but their zeal is merciless.  
To them, corruption is not a mistake; it is heresy.
