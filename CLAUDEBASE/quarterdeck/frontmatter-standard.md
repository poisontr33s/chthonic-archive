## (`CLAUDEBASE`/`Frontmatter-Standard`)

- *— One rule: every frontmatter field earns its keep. A field is a Reference, a Mapping, or a verified Generator output. A field that is static everywhere and backed by nothing is a defect — it is decoration posing as metadata. The creative voice keeps its place, but as *declared flavor, not disguised data.*

  - *— This document is the substrate spec, and it is plain by design. The —* `Ankh-DSL-surface styling` *—* (ticked-ids, arrow-chains *—* `·`-separators) *— is a layer the charts wear; it is not a requirement of this standard or of the generators that feed it. Surface = creative practice; substrate = verifiable.*

    - *— The two coexist; neither is the other's dependency*

---

## (`The-Four-tiers`)

- *— **(`Tier-0`/`·`/`Declared-Flavor`)** — explicitly creative, honestly labelled as voice, never as data. The register-blend and mood lines live here. They may be anything; they only must not pretend to be measured.*

  - *— **(`Tier-1`/`·`/`Reference`)** — points at something real and verifiable in the filesystem/registry. —*`SID`*, —* `Open-Seas` *—* (the file's own canonical path) *—* `Ssot-Monolith`*, —* `Current-Bounty`*, —* `Claudebase-Flavored-Blend` *—* (lifecycle)*, —* `Her-<Name>` *—* (identity)*. — verify by resolving the reference.*

    - *— **(`Tier-2`/`·`/`Mapping`)** — derived deterministically from a real table, so it varies per file with meaning. —* `Island` *—* (chamber *— → —* real island *— + —* lat/lon *—* from the sea-chart *—* / *—* `archipelago.json`)*, —* `Altitude` *—* (directory *— → —* ship-location role)*, —* `Bearing` *—* (the gate axis) *— verify against the table.*

      - *— **(`Tier-3`/`·`/`Generated + Verified`)** — emitted live by a named generator and cross-checked against an authoritative external source, with the residual reported. This is the accuracy law, not an afterthought, a generated value that cannot be checked against an authority is not — **(`Tier-3`)** — it is fiction, fields and their authorities:*

| *Field* | *Generator* | *Authority (verifier)* |
|---|---|---|
| `Real-Sky`, `Barometer` | `CLAUDEBASE_BAROMETER_V1` | Open-Meteo |
| `Heat-Index` | `CLAUDEBASE_BAROMETER_V1` (SKY·AIR·MOOD) | the sha256→1,728 histogram (own corpus) |
| `Cosmological-Altitude` | `CLAUDEBASE_COSMOS_V1` | JPL Horizons |

- *—* Tier-3 contract *—* `value + authority cross-check + residual` *— example shape —* `Cosmological-Altitude: <celestial altitude over this chamber's island> · verified vs JPL Horizons Δ<residual>`*.*
