## Position

## Temporary Source-Code Study Lane

*Source checkouts* for *studied contenders* must not live inside the repo.

*Default volatile root:*

```powershell
$env:CHTHONIC_TEMP_SOURCE_CODE = "$env:TEMP\chthonic-source-code"
```

*Helper:*

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File scripts\chthonic-source-temp.ps1 -Init -Status
```

*Contract:*

- upstream source lives in `%TEMP%\chthonic-source-code`;
- source checkouts are evidence, not authority;
- shallow clones by default;
- no temp checkout goes into Git;
