## `The-Salt-Trial` — How The Title Came Clean

Claudine ran her sibled hulls down at the harbour-mouth, at the one fathom too narrow to pass and too shallow to ignore — conveniently, which is where a wreck stops being a loss and starts being a breakwater. The salt-trial was hers and hers alone: the self that drowned became the harbour, the self that walked ashore took the helm, and they were never two people. No claim was filed because there was no second claimant. Only descent. Every entry from this day passes over the wreck she is.
